This Privacy Policy explains how Syncra ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our app.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account information**: name, email, profile photo, and bio you provide when signing up.
- **Content you share**: posts, stories, messages, reels, and media you upload.
- **Usage data**: app activity such as last-seen timestamps and presence status, used to power features like chat and online status.
- **Device information**: basic technical data needed to keep the app working reliably.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to operate core features (chat, posts, stories, calling), personalize your experience, keep your account secure, and improve the app.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use trusted third-party services to run the app, including:
- **Supabase** for authentication, database, and real-time features.
- **Cloudinary** for storing and delivering photos, videos, and other media.
- **LiveKit** for voice and video calling.

These providers only receive the data necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

## 4. Your Privacy Controls
You can control what others see about you at any time from **Settings → Privacy**, including your online status, who can see it, read receipts, and blocked accounts.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion
You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from **Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account**.

## 6. Your Rights
Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We'll let you know of any significant changes within the app.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have questions about this policy, reach out via **Settings → Support → Send Feedback**.
